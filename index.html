How to Play

Goal

Lowest total hand value when someone declares. Lowest cumulative score after all rounds wins.

J
Blind swap — swap one of your unseen cards with an opponent's unseen card (no peeking). OR if you already have J's in hand, drop the drawn J and drop your matching J's too.
Q
Choose one of your cards and one opponent card, see both, then optionally swap.

Same-rank Drop Chain

When you draw a card and discard it, you can also drop all matching cards of the same rank from your hand at once. Example: draw J, already have 2 J's in hand — discard drawn J and drop your 2 J's, going from 4 cards to 1 card.

Drop on Discard

After ANY player discards a card, you may drop your matching-rank cards if you know you have them. Wrong drop = keep it + receive 1 penalty card.

Start of Round

Each player gets 4 face-down cards. You peek your bottom 2 cards (cards 3 & 4) once — they are then permanently face-down until the round ends.

Declaring

On your turn, declare instead of drawing. If you have the lowest total — everyone scores their hand total. If not — you score your total + 25 penalty.
